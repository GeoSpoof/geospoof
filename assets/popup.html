Advanced

Runs protection at the very start of page loads, so sites have less time to read your real timezone. Asks for Firefox's “userScripts” permission.

Also sets your timezone in Chrome's engine, which reaches more of a page's workers and frames than standard protection. Chrome shows a debugging bar while it's on.
